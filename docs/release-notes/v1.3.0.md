## Whisper Push 1.3.0

### New: Voice Processing (macOS, off by default)

Dictating while your Mac plays a video, a podcast or a song? Turn on
**Settings → Voice Processing** in the menu bar. Whisper Push then records
through macOS's own voice processing (the one FaceTime uses): it removes what
the Mac itself is playing from the microphone signal, reduces background noise
and evens out your voice level. Everything stays on your Mac.

- **Off by default.** Nothing changes until you tick it. It applies from the
  next dictation, no restart needed.
- While it is on and you dictate, macOS may lower other apps' audio slightly;
  Whisper Push asks for the smallest reduction macOS allows (macOS 14+).
- If voice processing can't start on your setup, Whisper Push records the
  normal way. Dictation never fails because of this option.
- Not worth turning on with headphones: the Mac's audio doesn't reach the mic.

### Recording fixes (everyone)

- The last ~20–30 ms of each recording are no longer cut off, so a quick
  release doesn't clip the end of the last word.
- Abnormal peaks from the microphone (a knock on the laptop) are capped before
  transcription.
- The audio thread no longer waits on the mid-recording microphone check.

### Upgrading

Your settings are kept, and the new option starts off.

- **macOS, in the app:** menu bar → **⬆ v1.3.0 available** (or **Check for Updates…**).
  The app installs the update and relaunches itself.
- **Homebrew:** `brew update && brew upgrade --cask whisper-push`
  (first install: `brew install --cask remenby31/whisper-push/whisper-push`)
- **DMG:** download `Whisper-Push-macOS-arm64.dmg` below, quit Whisper Push and drag
  the new app over the old one in Applications.
- **Windows / Linux:** install the new `.msi` / `.deb` below over the old one.
